1. Laptops priced above $2000 require "Manager" approval.
2. Servers and networking equipment purchases require "IT Director" approval regardless of price.
3. Office furniture (Chair, Desk) under $500 is auto-approved by the system.
4. Any purchase from a vendor with a reliability rating below 4.0 must have "Finance" approval to mitigate risks.
5. Software licenses over $1000 require "Security Team" approval.
6. The maximum lead time allowed for urgent requests is 5 days.
7. Any purchase request must not exceed the department's remaining budget.
