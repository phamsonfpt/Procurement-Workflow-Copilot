# TechCorp Vietnam - Approval Authority Matrix
Document ID: POL-PROC-002
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 1.4

## 1. Standard Approval Thresholds
To balance operational efficiency with financial control, purchase requisitions (PRs) require approval based on their total estimated monetary value (in USD equivalent).

- **Tier 1: Under $1,000**
  - **Approver**: Auto-Approved (System Level)
  - **Conditions**: Must be within the allocated department budget and from a preferred vendor. Excludes restricted categories.
- **Tier 2: $1,000 to $10,000**
  - **Approver**: Line Manager
  - **Conditions**: Requires budget validation.
- **Tier 3: $10,001 to $50,000**
  - **Approver**: Department Head
  - **Conditions**: Requires at least 3 competitive quotes or a sole-source justification.
- **Tier 4: Over $50,000**
  - **Approver**: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)
  - **Conditions**: Requires a formal business case, full competitive tender process, and explicit CFO sign-off.

## 2. Special Category Approvals
Certain procurement categories carry additional risks or require specialized knowledge. These categories require secondary approvals regardless of the monetary value:
- **IT Equipment (Hardware/Infrastructure)**: Requires approval from the **IT Director**.
- **Software Licenses & SaaS Subscriptions**: Requires review and approval from the **Security Team** (to assess data privacy and cybersecurity risks) before final financial approval.
- **Consulting & Legal Services**: Requires approval from the **General Counsel** or **HR Director**.
- **Facilities & Office Modifications**: Requires approval from the **Facilities Manager**.

## 3. Emergency Procurement Procedures
In genuine emergencies where immediate action is required to prevent significant financial loss, operational downtime, or safety hazards:
- The standard approval matrix may be temporarily bypassed.
- Verbal or email approval from a Director or VP is sufficient to proceed.
- A retrospective PR must be created and fully documented within 48 hours of the emergency event, clearly marked as "Emergency Procurement."

## 4. Policy Enforcement
Any attempt to split a single large purchase into multiple smaller PRs (smurfing) to bypass approval thresholds is a violation of policy. The system will flag cumulative spending in the same category over a 30-day period for manual review.
