# TechCorp Vietnam - Purchase Requisition Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
Document ID: SOP-PROC-003
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.0

## 1. Purpose
This SOP details the step-by-step process for creating, submitting, and processing a Purchase Requisition (PR) to ensure all requests are handled systematically and efficiently.

## 2. Step-by-Step Workflow

### Step 1: Identify Need and Check Inventory
- **Action**: Before creating a PR, the Requester must verify that the required item is not already available in internal inventory or covered by an existing enterprise license.
- **SLA**: Immediate (Requester responsibility).

### Step 2: Create Purchase Requisition (PR)
- **Action**: The Requester logs into the Procurement System to create a new PR.
- **Requirements**:
  - Detailed specifications of the goods/services.
  - Accurate quantities required.
  - Required delivery date.
  - Estimated budget or confirmed quote amount.
  - Selection of the appropriate GL (General Ledger) code and cost center.

### Step 3: Attach Supporting Documents
- **Action**: The Requester uploads necessary documentation.
- **Requirements**:
  - For standard items under $5,000: Link to the vendor's catalog or a single quote.
  - For items over $5,000: Minimum of three (3) competitive quotes or a completed Sole Source Justification form.
  - Any required technical specifications, SOW (Statement of Work), or NDAs.

### Step 4: Submit for Approval
- **Action**: The PR is routed automatically based on the Approval Authority Matrix (POL-PROC-002) and Special Category Restrictions.
- **SLA**: System routing happens instantly upon submission.

### Step 5: Approval / Rejection Workflow
- **Action**: Approvers receive notifications and review the PR.
- **Outcomes**:
  - **Approve**: PR moves to the next approver or directly to the Procurement team.
  - **Reject**: PR is returned to the Requester with comments explaining the rejection.
  - **Request More Info**: PR is paused until the Requester provides necessary clarifications.
- **SLA**: Approvers are expected to action PRs within 48 hours.

### Step 6: Conversion to Purchase Order (PO)
- **Action**: Once fully approved, the PR is assigned to a Buyer.
- **Process**:
  - The Buyer validates the vendor, terms, and compliance.
  - The PR is converted into a legally binding Purchase Order (PO).
  - The PO is dispatched to the vendor electronically.
- **SLA**: Within 24 hours of final PR approval for standard items; up to 5 days for complex contracts.

## 3. Tracking and Visibility
Requesters can track the real-time status of their PR through the Procurement Workflow Copilot dashboard.
