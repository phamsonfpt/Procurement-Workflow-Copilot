# TechCorp Vietnam - Budget and Financial Controls
Document ID: POL-FIN-005
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 2.0

## 1. Annual Budget Allocation
Each department is allocated an annual budget approved by the Board of Directors prior to the start of the fiscal year (January 1 - December 31).
- The budget is divided into distinct Cost Centers and General Ledger (GL) categories (e.g., Software, Hardware, Travel, Marketing).
- Department Managers are accountable for managing expenditures within their allocated budget.

## 2. Budget Reservation and Checking
To prevent overspending, TechCorp Vietnam employs strict budget controls integrated into the procurement process.
- **Budget Check**: Before a Purchase Requisition (PR) can be submitted, the system automatically checks the remaining available budget for the selected Cost Center and GL code.
- **Reservation**: Upon PR submission, the estimated amount is temporarily "reserved" (encumbered) against the budget.
- **Commitment**: Once the PO is issued, the reserved amount becomes a firm commitment.

## 3. Over-Budget Procedures
If a PR exceeds the available budget for a specific category:
- The system will block submission.
- The Requester must request a **Budget Transfer** from another category with surplus funds, subject to Finance approval.
- Alternatively, a **Supplemental Budget Request** must be submitted, which requires a business case and approval from the CFO.

## 4. Budget Splitting (Smurfing) Prevention
Attempting to bypass approval thresholds by breaking a large purchase into multiple smaller transactions is strictly prohibited.
- **Control Rule**: If a requester submits multiple PRs within the same category that cumulatively exceed $500 within a 30-day rolling window, the auto-approval threshold is suspended.
- **Consequence**: All subsequent PRs in that category within the 30-day window will require, at minimum, Manager approval, regardless of the individual PR amount.

## 5. Expense Classification: CAPEX vs. OPEX
Proper financial classification is critical for tax and accounting purposes.
- **Capital Expenses (CAPEX)**: Purchases of assets that provide long-term value (over 1 year) and cost more than $2,500 (e.g., servers, company vehicles, significant office renovations). CAPEX requires specific depreciation tracking.
- **Operating Expenses (OPEX)**: Short-term, day-to-day operational costs (e.g., office supplies, SaaS subscriptions, consulting fees, standard employee laptops).
- Requesters must select the correct classification; the Finance Team will review and reclassify if necessary.
