# TechCorp Vietnam - Contract and Payment Terms
Document ID: POL-FIN-006
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 1.5

## 1. Standard Payment Terms
To optimize cash flow while maintaining strong vendor relationships, TechCorp Vietnam adheres to standardized payment schedules.
- **Standard Term**: **Net 60 days** from the date of a valid, accepted invoice.
- **Preferred Vendor Term**: **Net 30 days** (offered as an incentive for strategic partners).
- **Exceptions**: Any requests for advanced payments, cash-on-delivery (COD), or terms shorter than Net 30 require written approval from the Finance Director.

## 2. The 3-Way Matching Process
To prevent fraud and ensure we only pay for what we receive, Finance utilizes a strict 3-way matching process before releasing any payment. An invoice will only be cleared for payment when the following three documents match in terms of quantity and price:
1.  **Purchase Order (PO)**: The official order sent to the vendor.
2.  **Goods Receipt Note (GRN)**: Proof of delivery or service completion.
3.  **Invoice**: The bill provided by the vendor.

## 3. Goods Receipt Note (GRN) Procedures
- Upon delivery of goods or completion of services, the Requester or the Receiving Department must log into the system and create a GRN within 48 hours.
- The GRN must accurately reflect the quantity accepted and the condition of the items.
- Services require a signed timesheet, milestone sign-off, or acceptance certificate attached to the GRN.

## 4. Partial Delivery Handling
- If a vendor delivers an order in multiple shipments, the Requester must create a partial GRN for each shipment.
- Finance will only process payment for the quantities explicitly marked as received in the partial GRN. The PO remains open until final delivery.

## 5. Dispute Resolution
If discrepancies arise between the PO, GRN, and Invoice (e.g., incorrect pricing, damaged goods):
- Finance will place the invoice on a "Dispute Hold."
- The Procurement Team will contact the vendor to resolve the issue (e.g., request a credit note or a revised invoice).
- Payment timelines (e.g., Net 60) are paused during a dispute and restart only when a corrected invoice is received.

## 6. Warranty and Return Policies
- Contracts must stipulate a minimum 1-year warranty for all hardware and physical equipment unless otherwise negotiated.
- The Procurement Team is responsible for initiating return merchandise authorizations (RMAs) for defective goods and ensuring credit notes are promptly issued by the vendor.
