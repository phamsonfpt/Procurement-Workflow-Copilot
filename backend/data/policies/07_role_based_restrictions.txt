# TechCorp Vietnam - Department-Specific Procurement Restrictions
Document ID: POL-PROC-007
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Version: 3.1

## 1. Overview
To ensure operational needs are met without unnecessary expenditure, certain procurement categories are restricted based on the requesting department's core function. This policy defines what specific departments are authorized to request.

## 2. Department-Specific Allowances and Restrictions

### Engineering and Design Departments
- **Hardware Allowances**: Authorized to request high-performance computing equipment, including:
  - High-end Windows laptops/workstations (e.g., Dell Precision series).
  - Apple MacBooks (Pro models) if required for iOS development or specific design tools.
  - Specialized peripherals (e.g., drawing tablets, multiple high-resolution monitors).
- **Software Allowances**: Authorized to request specialized IDEs, CAD software, and enterprise design suites (e.g., Adobe Creative Cloud, AutoCAD).

### Marketing, HR, Sales, and Admin Departments
- **Standard Equipment**: Restricted to standard corporate equipment configurations (e.g., Dell Latitude series, standard dual monitors).
- **Exceptions for High-End Equipment**: Requests for MacBooks, high-end laptops, or gaming-tier hardware from these departments are generally prohibited.
  - **Override**: Exceptions require a detailed business justification demonstrating clear ROI, and must be approved by the Department VP and the IT Director.

## 3. Company-Wide Procurement Rules

### Office Supplies
- **Auto-Approval**: Requests for standard consumable office supplies (e.g., pens, paper, notebooks) totaling **under $100** are automatically approved by the system.
- Requesters must use the designated preferred vendor for office supplies (e.g., OfficeMax/Staples corporate catalog).

### IT Equipment Governance
- **Mandatory Review**: ALL purchases of IT hardware, networking equipment, or infrastructure—regardless of the monetary amount or the requesting department—MUST be routed through and approved by the **IT Director**. This ensures network compatibility, inventory management, and standardization.

### Software and Security Governance
- **Security Review**: ALL requests for new software licenses, SaaS subscriptions, or cloud services MUST undergo a review by the **Security Team**.
- **Purpose**: To verify SOC2 compliance, data residency, GDPR/PDPA compliance, and overall cybersecurity posture before any contract is signed or software is installed on the corporate network.
